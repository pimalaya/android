# Crash and surface hardening

Capabilities moved: mail (added: no message or calendar object can overflow the bridge's stack; a body whose reading kills the app is given up; a link in a message opens only when tapped; a failed native call answers an error; a killed run leaves nothing in the cache; modified: the bridge logs to logcat), calendar (added: expansion is bounded whatever the rule; the phone's calendars take bounded data), onboarding (added: nothing leaves the phone by backup or transfer; the sync adapters serve their own accounts alone). The WebView link check on a device is owed, so the change stays active.

**Why.** The 2026-10-11 audit (S1 M1, M2, L1, L4, L5, L6; S3 1, 3, 7; S4 L1 to L4; the JNI carry-over): a deeply nested message or calendar object overflowed a 1 MiB Java stack (SIGSEGV, a crash loop on a body still owed), a secondly rule or a huge count walked for minutes, a Graph series fanned out to 1481 requests, server calendar data reached the provider unbounded, a panic answered null, a message could open links on its own, a device transfer left the app failing every start, other apps could drive the exported sync adapters, a killed run left plaintext in the cache, and a release build logged debug records and addresses.

**Bridge.** mail.rs numbers sections with a stack of its own (`MAX_DEPTH` 64); `derivable` hands io-pimdir a message's header alone when its multipart tree runs deeper (a count of `multipart` spares the parse otherwise), in `mail::parse`, `pimdirDerive` and Gmail's `written`. calendar.rs `cst_of` refuses an object nesting components deeper than 16, folded `BEGIN`s counted, before ical-rs parses it; every bridge parse of an iCalendar object goes through it (calendar, occurrence, conflict, series, phone, jmap.rs). `Window::place` counts every walked occurrence against `MAX_WALKED` (200 000) and stops at `MAX_EXPANSION` (2 s); series.rs `remaining` takes at most `MAX_WALKED` of a `COUNT`; graph_calendar.rs `series_windows` clamps any series to `SERIES_YEARS` either side of today; phone.rs `hidden` lists secondly and minutely rules. ffi.rs `ErrorReply` answers `{"error": "Internal error: .."}` for a panic or a JNI failure (a pending exception cleared first), logs and defaults for `i32` and `()`; every export resolves with it, `closeMailSession`, `joinMailRun` and `pimdirVersion` now inside `with_env`, `JNI_OnLoad` under `catch_unwind`. logger.rs starts at warnings; `Native.debugLogs` (called from `Native`'s static block with the client's `BuildConfig.DEBUG`) turns the old levels on. `PimalayaClient.read` and `array` read a null reply as an internal error.

**App.** `MailPoison` (preferences `mail-poison`) marks a read with `commit` before `MailEngine.stored` derives and parses a body and before `MailReader.read` fetches and parses one, clearing it after; three marks left over poison the body: `MailEngine.download` skips and settles it, `MailList` says *Download failed*, the reader says `message_unreadable`. `MessageView.linkIntent` opens only a gesture in the main frame, `http`, `https` or `mailto`, with `CATEGORY_BROWSABLE` and no component or selector, and logs the scheme alone. res/xml/data_extraction_rules.xml and backup_rules.xml exclude every domain; `SecureStore.loadAll` drops accounts on an `AEADBadTagException`, `UnrecoverableKeyException` or `KeyPermanentlyInvalidatedException` (`keyLost`) and flags it (`takeLost`); `MainActivity.storedAccounts` asks to add them again (`accounts_lost`) or toasts a transient failure (`accounts_unreadable`). `SyncService.serves` and `CalendarSyncService.serves` gate on the app's account type and their authority. `CacheSweep` removes `MailEngine.SCRATCH` folders and `FilesList.STAGED` copies older than an hour on every start. `OpenedFiles.safe` drops control and format characters and cuts at 200 UTF-8 bytes between characters. `CalendarMapping` carries at most `MAX_REMINDERS` (5) and `clip`s title, location (`SHORT_TEXT`, 1024) and description (`LONG_TEXT`, 16 384). `Accounts.redacted` names an address by its domain in release warnings (Accounts, AccountSettings, MailBodies, SecureStore, MessageCompose, RemotePass, SyncRunner, the sync adapters).

**Edits outside this change's files.** ffi/oauth.rs (policy swap only), client/gmail.rs (`derivable`), jmap.rs (`cst_of`), and one log line in RemotePass, SyncRunner, AccountSettings, MessageCompose and MailBodies.

**Tests.** Rust (479 green): a 10 000-level message parsed, opened and derived on a 1 MiB thread; the depth guard; a three-level message numbered whole; 2 000 nested components, plain and folded, refused on a 1 MiB thread; SECONDLY from 1970 bounded, and capped at 1024 placed in its window; a split far into `COUNT=4294967295` bounded; Graph windows for 1601 to 9000, a short and an ended series; phone listing of secondly and minutely rules; the panic hint. Robolectric: `HardeningTest` (links, names, sync adapters, lost accounts, poisoned reads, the cache sweep, redaction), `CalendarMappingTest` (a hostile event bounded, a secondly series listed).

**Left open.** The device check of the reader's links (meta refresh, iframe). The OAuth loopback listener (S1 L2) stays with the network-trust change, which owns OauthFlow. io-pimdir, ical-rs, io-imap and io-webdav are fixed in their repositories; the bridge's guards stay until they are bumped. Account removal does not sweep `mail-poison` keys (a few bytes each).
