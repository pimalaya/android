# The dock's line names the domain only where a pass runs several

Capabilities moved: offline-store (modified: the dock's second line is the engine's step alone on a list's own pass, after the domain on the drawer's).

A list's pull or first sync runs its tab's domain, so *Syncing mail ·* repeated what the tab says and took the room the step needs. `MainActivity.renderSyncDock` drops it while the pass's sections cover one domain, keeps it on the drawer's sync, and shows the domain alone until a step is named. *Loading older mail* stays, naming the kind of pass rather than its domain.
