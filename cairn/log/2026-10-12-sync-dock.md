# The sync strip becomes a dock above the bottom bar

Capabilities moved: offline-store (modified: a sync shows in a dock on top of the bottom navigation of every list, the account over the domain and the engine's step, the count of the pass's accounts, one bar along its top edge, the extended add button rising above it; added: the dock opens onto a sheet of the pass's accounts and its last lines), mail (modified: the background download shows in the dock).

The app bar is back as it was before the strip moved into it: `bar_strip`, `bar_strip_line`, `bar_strip_glyph` and `bar_strip_bar` leave activity_main.xml, and a `sync_dock` sits between the flipper and `bottom_nav`, so the content ends above it and showing it moves neither the title nor the rows; a list scrolled to its end scrolls on by the dock's height, keeping *Load more* in sight. `SyncDock` holds one state for every list (the pass, a wait, a background run, the phone's first projection, the background download) where `ListHeader` kept one per list, shows only while a list is the screen (`onList`, from `showDomainBar` and `applyChrome`), and raises the extended FAB by its height. `MainActivity.renderSyncDock` builds what it shows: the account, or *Starting the sync* before one; the domain, *Loading older mail* for *Load more*, then the step (`SyncSteps.text`, the `sync_step_*` strings restored, the collections count for a pass, the bodies for a mail step); *1 of N* over the pass's sections, now kept in planning order with the ones reached. The sheet is a floating `Dialog` (`SyncSheet` style, sync_sheet.xml): title, percentage, bar, a row per section (running, next, done, named by domain when the pass spans several), and the pass's last 50 lines in memory, newest first with their time, dropped as the next pass starts; it closes on the scrim, its chevron, or the pass's end. The remaining mentions of the strip in spec/mail.md, spec/offline-store.md and spec/onboarding.md now say the dock. `SyncDockLayoutTest` replaces `SyncStripLayoutTest`: the title's and the content's tops do not move as the dock shows and hides, and the content ends at its top.
