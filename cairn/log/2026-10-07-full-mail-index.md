# Every mailbox's metadata is stored whole, and the list loads lazily

Capabilities moved: mail (added: a mailbox is stored whole, an account bounds its mail, the mail list loads lazily, a listing's text is decoded before it is stored, the attachment mark is corrected by the body; modified: the mail list narrows what it shows, the mail list selects, a message's date is its Date header; removed: an envelope's text is decoded before it is stored), offline-store (added: nothing reaches the store unnamed, a round lands page by page; removed: an unnamed handle is a probe).

Built on pimdir's joint plan `scoped-mail-sync`, whose io-pimdir (35a1c3f) and io-msgraph (00ef069) the bridge now takes as git dependencies pinned by rev; `deny.toml` already allowed the pimalaya organisation's git sources.

**Engine seam.** `offline.rs` speaks the paged seam: the `enumerate` yield carries `listing` (a delta from the checkpoint, or a round from its first page or a cursor, `band` for a widening) and `scope`, keeping a top-level `cursor` for the one-page connectors; a reply is a page (`last`, `cursor`, `checkpoint`) whose members carry their meta (`linkId`, `summary`, `sortKey`, a body where a listing read one), or `cursorRejected`. `offlineSync` takes the scope's floor and whether the connector's checkpoint is bound to it. The Java store services the round ops (`openRound`, `stamp`, `setRoundCursor`, `closeRound`, `setCoverage`) through the canonical statements, loads the coverage, the open round and the unstamped bindings, answers a load naming no handle with the sync state alone, and refuses an unnamed placement; the probe table and every read of it are gone, and the store reconciles a version-1 store whose draft dropped a table or added a column carrying a `CHECK`.

**Connectors.** IMAP lists `UID SEARCH` (`SENTSINCE` a day below the floor, `ALL` without one) once per session, then 500 UIDs a page by `UID FETCH (UID FLAGS RFC822.SIZE BODY.PEEK[HEADER.FIELDS (...)])`, the cursor the lowest UID under its UIDVALIDITY, the checkpoint taken at the first page's EXAMINE; a QRESYNC delta reads the headers of what moved. Graph lists its message delta filtered on `receivedDateTime`, 1,000 a page, the delta link closing the round; an expired link restarts it. JMAP pages `Email/query` by position and deltas by `Email/changes`, a message moved out being a removal. Gmail pages `messages.list` (`after:` two days below the floor) by page token, reading each id's metadata under the pacing, and deltas by history. Every member is named through io-pimdir's Annex A derivation or a summary built from the provider's fields; the date is the `Date` header everywhere, Gmail and JMAP included now (`Date` among the metadata headers, `sentAt`). The DAV, Google and Graph contacts and calendar drivers keep their one-page listings and are named in `PimdirEngine`: a member bound at an unchanged revision by the link id the store holds, any other by its body, read through the driver's own fetch 64 at a time.

**App.** `MailEngine.sync` is one engine run under the account's bound (`MailScope`, the first of the month N months back, kept by account id beside the store), with no meta upgrade after it, and tells the list after every write so a first pass shows page by page. `MailStore` reads `count_mail`, `count_mail_by_day`, `count_unread`, `list_mail_page_filtered`, `search_mail` and `list_coverage` with typed binds, an offset page reading the canonical statement as a subquery where no neighbouring page is loaded; `collect_before` and `recompute_refcounts` free what a narrowed bound leaves out. `MailList` is sized by the count, places its day headers from the per-day counts, reads 50 rows a page on its own thread around the scroll position (24 pages cached, a placeholder while one loads), keeps the outbox on top, and selects by store id, select-all being a query read whole only when the bar acts. The account settings offer all mail or the last 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 months. Opening a message corrects its attachment mark from the walk of its parts, which `parseMessage` now reports.

Not measured here: the benchmark moved to the joint plan's task 0, which measured Graph only.
