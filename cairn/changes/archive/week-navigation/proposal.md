# Week navigation and row touch-ups

## Why

The week card could only filter this week, leaving no way to look at another week. On the device, the unread dot sat off the paperclip's axis, the replied mark sat far from the sender it concerns, the empty states hid under the list headers, and the agenda's kind-of-entry glyphs needed a legend.

## What

- Week card: arrows to the weeks either side and the week's number, which brings the card back to this week. Another week with no day picked shows that whole week.
- Mail row: the replied mark before the sender; the paperclip at the edge of the subject's line, the star inside it, the dot above centred on the paperclip's column.
- Agenda row: the kind of entry by name, before how long it runs.
- Empty states centred in the space under the list's header.
