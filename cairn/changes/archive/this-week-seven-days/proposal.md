# This week shows its seven days

## Why

With no day picked, the current week listed every entry from today on, months ahead, while any other week listed its own seven days. The week card reads as a week, so this week should behave like the others.

## What

With no day picked, the agenda lists the shown week's seven days, from the locale's first weekday, this week included: its past days stay, nothing after Sunday shows. Entries are expanded over the shown week alone, since nothing else is listed.
