## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Google Tasks' limits are said
A task on a Google task list SHALL show, on its page, what Google Tasks keeps (title, notes, due day, done), and a tap SHALL open what it does not: the state, categories, priority, who it waits on and the mail it came from staying on this phone alone, the due time dropped, In progress and Cancelled folded to open and done, a task changed on both sides coming back as a whole-task conflict, a subtask made on Google under a task made here not shown under it. The account's Tasks row of a Google account SHALL open the same.

#### Scenario: A task on a Google list
- GIVEN a task on a Google task list
- WHEN its page opens
- THEN a row says what Google keeps, and a tap lists what stays on this phone
